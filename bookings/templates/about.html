Our Story

Desh Ghuri was created with one goal: make travel immersive and personal. After exploring various corners of the world, we realized the best experiences came from local guides and authentic human connections. So, in 2021, we launched a platform to bring that vision to life.

From a few destinations to a global network of passionate guides, we’ve grown steadily—while staying true to our commitment to authenticity, affordability, and fair practices.

Every trip booked through Desh Ghuri is more than a journey—it’s a cultural exchange that benefits both traveler and host. Our platform ensures guides are paid fairly and that travelers get genuine, heartfelt experiences.
